# Stakeholder Map: Product Feature Report

## Key Stakeholders and Interests

| Stakeholder | Interest | Source |
|-------------|----------|--------|
| Requester (author and reviewer of the report) | Gets a complete, plain-English report of every domain, feature and business rule, saved in `docs/`, that can be reviewed and updated later | [desc] [Q7] |
| Product owners and business analysts (first readers) | Understand the current system from the product perspective so they can plan changes to it | [Q1] [desc] |
| Product review or planning cycle participants | Use the report to decide what to change, keep or remove, with gaps and half-built features flagged | [Q2] [Q3] |

## Decision-Makers vs. Influencers

| Role | Who | Source |
|------|-----|--------|
| Decision-maker (reviews and signs off the report) | The requester, who reviews the report personally | [Q7] |
| Influencer (shapes what the report must explain) | Product owners and business analysts, as the first readers | [Q1] |

## Communication Requirements

| Requirement | Detail | Source |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| Review and sign-off | The requester reviews the report and approves each step of the writing | [Q7] [desc] |
| Refresh cadence | The report is refreshed when the system changes | [Q7] |
| Reviewable layout | One file per domain plus an index page, so each domain can be reviewed and updated on its own | [Q6] [desc] |

None.
