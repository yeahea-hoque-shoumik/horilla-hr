# Glossary

## Summary

This page explains, in plain English, every term written in bold anywhere in this report. Terms are in alphabetical order. Each entry starts with the term in bold, followed by its meaning.

## Terms

- **Active company**: the company a user has chosen to work in at the top of the screen. Most lists show only that company's data.
- **API**: a way for other software, such as the company's mobile app, to read and change data in the system without using its screens.
- **Archive**: hiding a record from normal use without deleting it. An archived record keeps its history and can be brought back.
- **Audit history**: a record of who changed what and when, kept for chosen kinds of record.
- **Background job**: a task the system runs on its own on a timetable, for example every 4 hours or every day at a set time, without anyone pressing a button.
- **Bold**: text in heavy type. In this report it marks a term explained in this glossary.
- **Bug**: a flag type. The system does something different from what it plainly intends.
- **Conflict**: a day on which an employee has both attendance and approved leave. HR must decide which one counts before payroll can run for that employee.
- **Cost to company (CTC)**: the total yearly or monthly cost of an employee to the employer, including pay and the employer's own contributions.
- **Cross-site scripting**: an attack in which someone types harmful code into a text box so that it runs in other people's browsers. The system checks text for it when saving.
- **CTC Down**: a salary structure mode in which the contract wage is the whole package and the pay items divide it up.
- **Eligibility**: conditions an employee must meet before a leave type applies to them, such as gender, department or length of service.
- **Employee record**: the system's record of one person working for the company, linked to their login and to almost every other record.
- **Employer contribution**: an amount the employer pays on top of an employee's pay, for example to a pension or provident fund, alongside a deduction taken from the employee.
- **Filing status**: a set of income tax rules (tax bands, deductions, rebates) assigned to an employee's contract.
- **Flag**: a marker in this report for something a product owner should know about, such as a bug, a gap or a risk.
- **Fork**: a company-owned copy of an open-source product that is then changed separately from the original.
- **Gap**: a flag type. Something a product owner would expect is missing.
- **Geofence**: an area around the office, set by a location and a radius. Check-in can be limited to people inside it.
- **Grace time**: how many minutes after a shift starts someone can arrive (or before it ends someone can leave) without being counted late or early.
- **Gross Up**: a salary structure mode in which the contract wage is basic pay and allowances are added on top of it.
- **Half-built**: a flag type. A feature was started or partly connected but not finished.
- **Hours balance**: for each employee and month, the hours worked against the hours required, counting only validated days.
- **Inconsistent**: a flag type. The same rule behaves differently depending on the screen or channel used.
- **Key result**: a measurable outcome that shows progress towards an objective.
- **Leave accrual**: earning leave little by little over time, for example a day and a half each month, instead of receiving it all at once.
- **Multi-level approval**: an approval chain in which several managers approve a request one after another.
- **Night shift**: a shift that starts later in the day than it ends, for example 22:00 to 06:00.
- **Objective**: a goal set for the company or for an employee in a period, tracked through key results.
- **Overtime**: time worked beyond the day's minimum working hours.
- **Penalty**: a consequence of arriving late or leaving early, either a fine taken from pay or a cut in leave days.
- **Permission**: a right to do a specific thing in the system, such as "change leave requests". Permissions are given through role groups.
- **Probation**: a trial period at the start of employment, with an end date set during onboarding.
- **Replace**: handing everything an employee is responsible for (such as being someone's reporting manager) over to another employee.
- **Reporting manager**: the person named on an employee's work information as their manager. This is who approves most of the employee's requests.
- **Risk**: a flag type. A feature works, but can give a wrong or surprising result in realistic conditions.
- **Role group**: a named group of permissions, such as "HR Manager" or "Payroll Manager", given to users.
- **Scheduler**: the separate process that runs the background jobs. If it is not running, no background job runs.
- **Superuser**: an all-powerful administrator account that passes every permission check.
- **Switched off**: a flag type. A feature ships with the product but is turned off or not installed.
- **Time zone**: the local time used for a place, such as Asia/Dhaka. Companies and employees can each have their own.
- **White-labelling**: showing a company's own name and branding in the system instead of the product's.
- **Work information**: the part of an employee record that holds their company, department, job position, shift, work type, reporting manager, joining date, salary and time zone.
